Golden Star V61 — Test IIF defaults

Based on V60.
Accounts Receivable and Cost of Goods Sold are fixed values in the QuickBooks PDF converter.
Invoice numbers automatically generate five digits followed by -test; invoice groups are sequential.
The sequence is kept in browser localStorage only after an IIF download; it is NOT connected to QuickBooks. It cannot prevent duplicates across computers, browser resets, or manual QuickBooks invoices. Verify against QuickBooks before import.
Cost of Goods Sold is generally an EXPENSE account, not an income account. These settings are requested for TESTING only and must be validated by the accountant before production use.
No invoice template selection. No Supabase changes.
